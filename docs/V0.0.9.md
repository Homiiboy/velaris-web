# Velaris V0.0.9 — Player Experience

V0.0.9 redesigns the full-screen video OSD so playback feels like a native part of Velaris while preserving the established Jellyfin-compatible playback engine underneath.

## Scope

- dedicated `velaris-player-page` shell for full-screen video playback
- subtle Velaris brand treatment while the OSD is visible
- cinematic bottom scrim and centered translucent control surface
- stronger title, secondary media info and playback-status hierarchy
- compact cyan-accented timeline with clearer time labels
- primary Play/Pause action separated visually from rewind/forward and secondary utilities
- refined subtitles, audio, volume, settings, casting, picture-in-picture and fullscreen controls
- premium Up Next and SyncPlay surfaces
- responsive layouts for desktop, mobile and TV
- keyboard/remote focus states and reduced-motion support

## Compatibility

Playback behavior is intentionally unchanged. Existing play/pause, seeking, chapters, next/previous media, subtitles, audio tracks, volume, settings, AirPlay, picture-in-picture, fullscreen, SyncPlay and Up Next logic remain controlled by the existing playback controller and media-player stack.

V0.0.9 is a presentation-layer milestone. It does not introduce a replacement decoder, transcoder, streaming protocol or playback backend.
